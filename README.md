## ⚡ About Me

<img align="right" width="150" alt="Hamza Manzoor Profile" src="images/hamza.png" style="border-radius: 50%; border: 3px solid #0d9488; margin-left: 20px;" />

I am a **Senior Full-Stack Engineer** with **6+ years of experience** designing, building, and shipping production-grade SaaS platforms. My current focus is on **Generative AI integration** and **LLM-powered product features**. 

*   🚀 **Backend Architectures:** Specialized in event-driven microservices (NestJS, Node.js, FastAPI) and job queue processing (BullMQ, Redis).
*   🎨 **Frontend Excellence:** Building performant, responsive single-page apps using React, Next.js, Redux Toolkit, and custom design systems.
*   🧠 **AI Orchestration:** Deep integration of OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini APIs, prompt chaining, RAG pipeline engineering, and GPU inference containerization.
*   🛠️ **Open Source:** Creator of WhatsHush (WhatsApp privacy tool) and Logaura (Node.js colored logger).
